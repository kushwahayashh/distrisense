Problem Statement 2: Distribution Intelligence & Inventory Optimization Engine
FMCG companies distribute products across multiple warehouses, distributors, and retail stores.
However, differences in regional demand, inventory availability, transportation costs, and
product shelf life make efficient distribution challenging. Some locations experience stockouts
while others hold excess inventory, resulting in lost sales, increased costs, and product wastage.
Your challenge is to develop a Ai Powered Distribution Intelligence Engine that helps
companies make smarter product distribution and inventory allocation decisions.
The solution should analyse available inventory, estimated demand, and distribution constraints
to recommend which products should be sent to which locations, in what quantities, and from
which warehouses. It should aim to minimise stockouts, unnecessary transportation costs, and
inventory wastage while making the best possible use of available stock.
Teams are encouraged to incorporate demand forecasting, inventory optimisation, or other
intelligent decision-making techniques to improve their recommendations.
Expected Outcome: A functional software solution that generates actionable product
distribution recommendations and demonstrates how its decisions can improve supply-chain
efficiency.
Note: Teams may use publicly available data or create realistic simulated datasets. All
assumptions should be clearly stated.
